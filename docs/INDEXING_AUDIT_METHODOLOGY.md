## 6. Actionable Policy Recommendations for PCCOE

1. **Preprint Differentiation**: Ensure the **91 papers hosted on Zenodo, SSRN, and Research Square** are classified as Open Working Papers/Preprints rather than journal publications in departmental appraisals.
2. **Immediate Purge of Pay-to-Publish Outlets**: Faculty members should be strictly advised against submitting to operations like *IJSREM* (10 papers), *IJRASET* (9 papers), and *IJFMR* (5 papers), which charge publication fees with non-existent or 48-hour peer reviews.
3. **Discontinued Journal Alert**: Alert faculty that *International Journal of Drug Delivery Technology* has been officially dropped by Scopus.
4. **Promotion of Q1/Q2 & IEEE/Springer Venues**: With **35 Q1 papers**, **13 Q2 papers**, and **over 200 IEEE/Springer proceedings**, the department possesses substantial high-quality research that can be spotlighted for NIRF criteria 3.1 & 3.2.
